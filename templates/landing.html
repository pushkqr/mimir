security protocol

Zero-Trust Geofencing.

Government policies belong on the intranet. Mimir features a built-in mathematical geofencing perimeter that inspects the client's host IP before a connection is even established.

  • Blocks external public traffic at the perimeter by default.
  • Isolated Token Registry maps sessions to hashed identities, eliminating password vulnerabilities.
